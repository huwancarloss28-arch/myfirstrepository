About Field Response

Knowledge That Supports Service

FIELD RESPONSE is a digital journal created to promote learning and professional development in disaster response and humanitarian assistance.

The journal brings together articles, educational resources, photographs and field activities that highlight preparedness, teamwork, leadership and service to affected communities.

  • ✓ Disaster preparedness and response
  • ✓ Humanitarian assistance
  • ✓ Military leadership development
  • ✓ Community resilience
  • ✓ Lessons learned and professional education
